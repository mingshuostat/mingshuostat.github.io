Hello! Welcome to my academic homepage and photography gallery.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University, where I work with Professor [Lu Tian](https://statistics.stanford.edu/people/lu-tian). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of California, Davis in 2026, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor [Hao Chen](https://anson.ucdavis.edu/~haochen/index.html) and Professor [Miles Lopes](https://anson.ucdavis.edu/~melopes/). I am also grateful to the senior collaborator and close friend [Doudou Zhou](https://doudouzhou.github.io/) for his encouragement. Before my Ph.D. studies, I earned my B.S. in Statistics from Southern University of Science and Technology in 2021.
 
My research develops statistical methods and theory for complex and high-dimensional data, with a particular interest in graph-based nonparametric testing and robust inference. More recently, I have been working on transfer learning and causal inference, including methods that use external data to improve survival analysis in randomized trials. I am also interested in generating and calibrating synthetic populations by combining individual-level data with aggregate information.

Alongside these statistical projects, I also work on AI for mathematics and autonomous research agents. In particular, my collaborators and I are developing a framework for AI-assisted mathematical and statistical research. Through our Math Engineering project, we are exploring how to organize research questions, conjectures, proofs, and computational evidence into a structured workflow that supports human–AI collaboration. Our goal is to make AI-assisted research easier to inspect, verify, and build upon.


Outside of research, I enjoy sports and photography, and I am deeply fascinated by the stars and the universe. You can find some of my photography [here](https://mingshuostat.github.io/photograph/).
